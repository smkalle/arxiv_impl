Use Case Why Now Key Risk Composite

Multimodal Product Discovery Single fused encode for text+image+video. Maps directly to Rakuten Ichiba search intent diversity. Video embedding latency; pre-extract frames for <500ms SLA 8.3 / 10
Support Ticket RAG Nano ≤4GB VRAM. Embeds screenshots alongside text. Directly extends SIRA KB prototype. Eval harness needed; use MIEB-Lite for image retrieval accuracy gates 8.3 / 10
Catalog Quality Scoring Cross-modal cosine detects image–title mismatches. Enhances CatalogLab CQS natively. Threshold calibration per category; needs labelled mismatch dataset 8.0 / 10
Video Recommendation No manual keyframe tagging pipeline. Strong engagement lift signal for commerce. Frame sampling quality varies; latency budget requires pre-extraction 7.8 / 10
Zero-shot Classification Built-in adapter, no training data. Good internal tool / moderation demo. Accuracy ceiling without fine-tune; may lose to task-specific models 7.7 / 10
Research Paper Search Native PDF + figure + audio in one index. Matryoshka 128-dim keeps cost low. Narrow buyer (academia); market size limits commercial upside 7.3 / 10
Audio Matching Unique capability; few competitors at this price point. librosa + Whisper dependency chain; niche TAM; MAEB benchmarks immature 6.0 / 10
